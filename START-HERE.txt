HOUSTON SURVIVAL MAP — laptop quick start
=========================================

1. Install Node.js (free, from nodejs.org) — only needed once.
2. Open a terminal in this folder and type:

       node scripts/serve.mjs 8080 app

3. Open http://localhost:8080 in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
4. Tap "More" -> "Map areas & storage" -> "Download" (this copies the map
   into the browser so it works without internet).
5. Done. Turn off Wi-Fi and try it. Keep step 2 running while you use it
   (localhost works with no internet).

Note: most laptops have no GPS chip. With no internet, the laptop may not
know where you are — pick "Start from the map center" or a saved place.
Phones have GPS that works offline.
